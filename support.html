Frequently asked

How does speed work? Speed is in words per minute (WPM), 150 by default. Change it with − / + before or during reading. The editor shows the word count and an approximate duration.

The text runs too fast. Drag the text back down with your finger to re-read; it starts again smoothly a moment after you let go. Tap the text to pause.

Does the text appear in my video? No. The script is only on screen; the recording contains just the camera image and your voice.

Which files can I import? .txt, .md, .rtf, .pdf (with real text, not scanned) and .docx. You can also paste text.

What is free? Your first 3 kept clips are free, in full quality and without a watermark. Rehearsing without recording is always free. After that, Prompter Plus Premium unlocks unlimited recordings (monthly, yearly or lifetime).

Restore or cancel a purchase. Use Settings → Restore purchases in the app. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

My clip doesn't open on Windows. Turn on Style → Most compatible format to save H.264 MP4.
