Privacy Policy

App: Prompter Plus (iOS) · Bundle identifier: com.themejunky.prompter
Developer: APLICATII ANDROID PLUS SRL · Last updated: October 23, 2026

SupportRomână
Summary. Your scripts and your recordings stay on your iPhone. Prompter Plus has no accounts, shows no ads and does not sell your data. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off at any time in the app's Settings.

Camera and microphone

Prompter Plus uses the camera and microphone only while the prompter screen is open, to show the live preview and to record the clips you start. Recording happens on your device. Video and audio are never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.

Your scripts and recordings

Scripts you paste, type or import, and the clips you record, are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. A clip leaves the app only when you choose to: Save to Photos adds it to your photo library (with your permission), and Share sends it to the destination you pick (for example AirDrop, Files or a messaging app). Files you import are read once to extract their text; the original file is not kept or uploaded.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the app we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect anonymous information such as which features are used (for example "a script was created" or "a recording was saved"), app version, device model, iOS version, country/region derived from network information, and diagnostic data when the app crashes. This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, it never includes the text of your scripts or your recordings, and it is not used for advertising. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share usage data inside the app. Google processes this data under its own terms: Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether Premium is active. A small counter of the free clips you have used is stored in your device's Keychain.

Children

Prompter Plus is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes your scripts and recordings from the app (clips you saved to Photos stay in Photos). If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK you have the rights given by the GDPR; for any request or question write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Politica de confidențialitate (rezumat în română)

Scripturile și clipurile tale rămân pe iPhone-ul tău. Prompter Plus nu are conturi, nu afișează reclame și nu vinde date. Camera și microfonul sunt folosite doar cât timp ecranul prompterului e deschis, iar înregistrarea se face pe telefon. Un clip pleacă din aplicație doar când alegi tu: Salvează în Poze sau Trimite.

Pentru a îmbunătăți aplicația folosim Firebase Analytics și Crashlytics (Google): statistici anonime de utilizare și rapoarte de erori, fără textul scripturilor, fără înregistrări, fără identificatorul de reclame și fără urmărire între aplicații. Le poți opri oricând din Setări → Trimite date de utilizare. Plățile sunt gestionate exclusiv de Apple. Pentru orice întrebare: contact@chrisbarin.com.
